About Afriqo Tanzania

Education, built for Tanzania.
Powered by technology.

Afriqo E-Learning Tanzania is an Arusha-based education technology company. We build and operate the platforms, programs and services that Tanzanian learners, educators and institutions actually use day-to-day.

From Standard 1 to the workplace, from a child's first robot to a company's digital transformation — we make education and technology meet, in a way that works here.

Our mission

To empower individuals and organizations with innovative e-learning solutions that enhance knowledge, skills and opportunities for personal, academic and professional growth.

Our vision

To become Tanzania's leading hub for digital education and professional development — shaping a smarter, skilled, future-ready generation through technology-driven learning.
